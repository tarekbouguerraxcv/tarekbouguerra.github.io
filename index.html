Tarek Bouguerra
About

Between acoustic
and electronic.

Tarek Bouguerra is an Italian composer, sound designer and performer. He honed his craft at the Civica Scuola di Musica Claudio Abbado in Milan, specialising in Applied Music.

His practice blends electroacoustic instruments with advanced sound processing techniques, creating original soundscapes across film, dance, theatre and interactive media.
